## Overview

The Fiat page uses Onramp so you can buy (or sell) supported assets with local payment methods.

## Steps

1. Open **Fiat** from the Trade menu.
2. Connect the wallet that should receive funds.
3. Choose buy or sell, then follow the Onramp widget.
4. When the transfer settles, return to **Swap** or **Portfolio** to use your balance.

## Notes

- Availability depends on your region and Onramp's supported rails.
- Dex223 swaps stay on Dex223. Use the Swap page for crypto-to-crypto trades.
