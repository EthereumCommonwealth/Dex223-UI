## What it does

The converter wraps or unwraps a token between ERC-20 and ERC-223 at a **1:1** rate with no protocol fee.

## Steps

1. Open **Token converter** from the More menu.
2. Select the token and the direction (to ERC-20 or to ERC-223).
3. Enter the amount and confirm in your wallet.

## When to use it

- You received ERC-223 but a dapp only accepts ERC-20 (or the reverse).
- You are preparing liquidity or a swap that expects a specific standard.
