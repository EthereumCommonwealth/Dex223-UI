## Before you start

Connect a wallet that holds the token you want to sell, and enough gas for the network you selected.

## Steps

1. Open **Swap** from the Trade menu.
2. Choose the token you pay with and the token you receive.
3. Enter an amount. Review the quoted price and price impact.
4. Confirm the swap in your wallet when prompted.

## Tips

- Prefer liquid pools for tighter quotes.
- If a token is missing, import it from **Token lists** or paste its contract address.
- ERC-20 and ERC-223 versions of the same asset may both appear. Pick the standard your wallet and destination expect.
